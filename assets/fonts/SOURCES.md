# Fonts used by the interface

Both variable fonts are self-hosted and distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- **Exo 2** — interface, including Cyrillic text. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/exo2. Local file: `Exo2.ttf`, weights 100–900. License: `OFL-Exo2.txt`.
- **Oxanium** — sample numbers, transport time and English section names. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oxanium. Local file: `Oxanium.ttf`, weights 200–800. License: `OFL-Oxanium.txt`.

The site makes no font requests to Google or another CDN.
