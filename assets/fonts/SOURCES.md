# Fonts used by the interface

The active typefaces are self-hosted IBM Plex fonts, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- **IBM Plex Sans**, regular and medium — interface, section names and captions. Local files: `IBMPlexSans-Regular.woff2`, `IBMPlexSans-Medium.woff2`.
- **IBM Plex Sans Condensed**, medium — sample numbers and the selected sound in the player. Local file: `IBMPlexSansCondensed-Medium.woff2`.

Source: https://github.com/IBM/plex. License: `OFL-IBMPlex.txt`.

Previously used Exo 2 and Oxanium files remain in the repository with their licenses, but are not requested by the page:

- Exo 2: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/exo2, `OFL-Exo2.txt`.
- Oxanium: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oxanium, `OFL-Oxanium.txt`.

The site makes no font requests to Google or another CDN.
